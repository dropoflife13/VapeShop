# Implementation Plan — VapeShop SaaS (`PM-09`)

1. **Phase 0 — Tenant & Regulatory Foundation (`TKT-001`, `TKT-002`):** Shop self-registration, 4-role AuthZ, PH Legal IDs, and DTI Banned Products gate.
2. **Phase 1 — Catalog, Suppliers & Append-Only Ledger (`TKT-003`, `TKT-004`):** Minimalist supplier ETA tracking, `P0001` ledgers, and `available_inventory_view`.
3. **Phase 2 — Serial QR Provenance & Editable Reservations (`TKT-005`, `TKT-006`):** Manufacturer serial posting + QR display, and 1-hour auto-expiring editable reservations.
4. **Phase 3 — POS Checkout & Role Consoles (`TKT-007`, `TKT-008`):** POS with 18+ ID gate, Shop Owner toggles, and System Admin repair/compliance console.
5. **Phase 4 — Hardening (`TKT-009`):** Full adversarial `/harden` verification.
